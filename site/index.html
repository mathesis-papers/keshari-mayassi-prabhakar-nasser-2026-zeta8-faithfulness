Extending Keshari–Mayassi–Prabhakar–Nasser's unfaithfulness theorem: a negative answer to their zeta8 faithfulness question

Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Published paper · Version 2 · CC BY 4.0

PDFSourceCitationRepositoryMathesis recordDOILean source

Abstract

Extending Keshari, Mayassi, Prabhakar and Nasser's unfaithfulness theorem (May 13, 2026, arXiv:2605.13090v1, Theorem 4.2(b) and Question 4.3, page 17), we answer their Question 4.3 negatively on three strands with at least three virtual colors: an infinite-order commutator has identity image for every allowed parameter, including a outside {0,1,-1}. We determine the kernel on the same-position virtual subgroup by exponent sums; its intersection over all parameter choices is the derived subgroup of a free group of rank k-1. The obstruction also applies to both associated subgroups and all eight homogeneous 2-local families. A related construction strengthens Nasser–Ocampo's welded unfaithfulness result by giving an infinite-order kernel element on three strands with at least two virtual colors.
